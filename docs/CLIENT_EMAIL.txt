WHAT WAS DELIVERED
- Weekly competitions per subject with a full five-stage lifecycle.
- Handwritten summary uploads, hidden during submission and shown anonymously during voting.
- Up to 3 votes for different summaries; fewer than 3 is allowed; votes lock permanently.
- No self-voting, no duplicate votes, and hidden tallies until approval.
- Historical vote tie-breaker and joint first place, with 3 points each and the prize held until a mutual settlement is recorded.
- 10 EGP contribution tracking, prize pool, financial ledger, CSV export, and manual settlements.
- Approved summary library with a default Book Reader (two-page desktop, single-page mobile), locked author attribution, PDF downloads, and bulk ZIP.
- Per-subject and overall Hall of Fame, annual points, and 404 Annual Champion.
- Badges separated from roles, RBAC, audit log, and in-app notifications.

PLEASE TEST (30-40 MINUTES, PREFERABLY IN INCOGNITO)

1. STUDENT (STU1001)
- Open the League.
- Open "أعلاف الدواجن والطاقة" (voting open). Confirm the summaries are anonymous, your own summary is blocked, select 2 summaries, confirm, and verify the votes lock.
- Open "هضم الألياف" (submission open), upload 1-2 images, and submit.
- Open the Library and read a summary in the Book Reader. Switch to Standard View and back, download a PDF, then test "Download all" (ZIP).
- Check Hall of Fame and My Achievements.

2. SUMMARY EDITOR (STU1004)
- Confirm the editor sees only the review queue and cannot approve results or publish summaries.

3. GROUP ADMIN (ADMIN-404)
- Open League > Administration.
- Results: open "البروتين في علائق الدواجن", verify the 7 vs 7 historical tie and joint first place, then approve.
- Finance: record the joint-winner settlement (amount, recipients, mandatory notes) and verify the ledger.
- Roles: grant/revoke Summary Contributor and appoint/remove Summary Editor (a reason is required).
- Publishing: log in as a joint winner (STU1002 or STU1004), submit the winning summary digitally, then publish it as admin.
- Audit: confirm the new entries.
- Optional: close the academic year to see 404 Annual Champion, then use "Reset demo data" to restore the seed.

4. MOBILE
- Check the League tabs, competition cards, single-page Book Reader, and PDF/ZIP downloads at about 390px width.
